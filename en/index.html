About

Computer vision engineer, at MTS since 2019: the CV group of the Machine Learning department at MTS AI, the competence center, the Computer Vision Center — from engineer to Principal Computer Vision Engineer. Along the way I shipped more than 15 systems to production — from OCR for a national library to video moderation and industrial defect detection — with synthetic datasets and augmentation pipelines as a specialty. Since July 2026 I lead the Applied Solutions Center at MWS AI.

I teach in parallel. At HSE I run MLOps, computer vision, advanced video analytics and GPU computing, teach at the Center for Top AI Specialists, supervise graduation theses — 21 defended in two years — and serve as deputy head of the Robotics Lab: perception systems, semantic navigation, SLAM, plus the team, budget and industry partnerships. At MIPT I am an associate professor at the Higher School of Software Engineering, and for Yandex Lyceum I designed an AI robotics course. In 2025 and 2026 the students of the Faculty of Computer Science voted me Best Teacher, and in 2025 my colleagues at MWS named me Teacher of the Year.

Education: Bauman Moscow State Technical University, technical physics (2013–2017); master’s in management, Moscow International Academy (2022–2024); Stanford’s Machine Learning course on Coursera (2019). Five physics publications, including Journal of Physics: Conference Series and Journal of Surface Investigation: X-ray, Synchrotron and Neutron Techniques. English at C1. I organise meetups and conferences and mentor young engineers.
